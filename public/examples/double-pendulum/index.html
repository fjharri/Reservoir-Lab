WHAT IS BEING TESTED

Adapt before the next reading.

Shared reservoir

The adaptive and frozen forecasts use the same sparse reservoir state. This controls for the motion becoming naturally easier: their only difference is whether the linear readout keeps learning.

Online learning

Recursive least squares updates the six-output adaptive readout after every observation. Its frozen twin retains the weights learned under the original physics.

The deadline

At the default 60 Hz the complete update has 16.7 milliseconds. Responses arriving later count as missed deadlines, while recovery requires two continuous seconds back in the pre-change error range.

Interpretation

A widening gap between frozen and adaptive error is direct evidence that online updates—not an easier trajectory—produce the recovery. It does not claim that every other model would fail.
