WHAT IS BEING TESTED

Adapt before the next reading.

Shared reservoir

The adaptive and frozen forecasts use the same sparse reservoir state. Their only difference is whether the linear readout keeps learning after the engineered parameter switch.

Online learning

Recursive least squares updates the adaptive readout after every delivered observation. The scheduler can drop observations when it falls more than four ticks behind rather than quietly slowing simulated time.

The deadline

At 60 Hz each response has 16.7 milliseconds from its scheduled sample time—not merely from dispatch. The audit also reports achieved rate, late ticks, dropped observations and total wall-clock drift.

Interpretation

The combined default is an intentionally strong stress test, not a representative physical event. Recovery requires returning to the prior range and remaining at least 25% below frozen for two seconds.
